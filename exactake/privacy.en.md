## 2. What Exactake Does

Exactake is a local-first nutrition tracking app. It helps users log food,
review nutrition estimates, calculate targets, track progress, and manage local
exports and deletion.

Exactake is provided for wellness and informational nutrition tracking only. It
is not a medical device, diagnosis tool, treatment tool, eating-disorder
intervention, or substitute for professional medical, nutrition, or mental
health advice.

## 3. Data Processed On Your Device

The current version stores app data locally on your device. This may include:

- profile, language, region, unit, and app-setting choices;
- diary entries, meal timing, quantities, nutrient snapshots, and summaries;
- foods, custom foods, recipes, meal templates, barcodes, source metadata, and
  source confidence;
- body measurements, progress entries, nutrition targets, formulas, and manual
  target overrides;
- local consent choices, local entitlement or sandbox purchase states, and
  local product-metric counters used for debugging or product evaluation;
- import, export, and local deletion state.

This local app data can be sensitive because it may reveal eating habits, body
measurements, goals, and health-adjacent preferences. Keep exported files,
device backups, and screenshots secure.

## 4. What Exactake Does Not Currently Do

The current production scope does not include:

- Exactake accounts;
- cloud sync;
- community public writes;
- production ads or personalized advertising;
- production in-app purchases or remote receipt validation;
- remote analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDKs;
- hosted OCR or upload of nutrition-label images;
- upload of diary entries, body measurements, nutrition targets, custom foods,
  recipes, exports, or account identifiers to an Exactake backend.

If these capabilities are added later, this Privacy Policy and the app-store
privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released.

## 5. Open Food Facts Barcode Lookup

When you use a barcode lookup and the product is not already available locally,
Exactake may send the normalized barcode over HTTPS to Open Food Facts in order
to retrieve public food-product data.

This lookup is user-triggered and is used for app functionality. Exactake does
not send diary entries, body measurements, target values, custom food names,
recipes, account identifiers, payment data, or Exactake user IDs with this
lookup.

Open Food Facts is an independent third-party service. Its own policies apply
to requests it receives.

## 6. Local Export, Import, and Deletion

Exactake lets you export local app data. Export files can contain sensitive
nutrition and body-related information. You control where those files are saved
or shared.

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the App, clear
app storage through the operating system, or use the in-app local-delete
workflow. The in-app local-delete workflow requires the exact confirmation text
`DELETE LOCAL DATA` before local records are cleared.

Because the current app does not provide Exactake accounts or cloud sync, there
is currently no remote Exactake account-deletion process.

## 7. Legal Basis For EEA Processing

For users in the European Economic Area, the current processing is based on:

- performance of a contract or pre-contractual app functionality for local app
  operation and user-requested barcode lookup;
- legitimate interests for basic local app reliability, security, and
  troubleshooting where no remote telemetry is used;
- consent where the operating system or app asks for an optional permission or
  optional future capability.

## 8. Third-Party Sources and Attribution

Exactake may display data from public food and nutrition sources such as Open
Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central where available. Source names, source URLs,
external identifiers, confidence, completeness, and license metadata may be
shown in the app to help users review where a value came from.

Exactake does not claim that third-party food data is complete, current, or
error-free.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

You can review, correct, export, import, or delete local app data through the
App's local controls where supported.

If you are in the EEA or another privacy-rights jurisdiction, you may have
rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability,
withdrawal of consent, and complaint to a supervisory authority. Because the
current app is local-first and does not operate an Exactake account backend,
many requests can be completed directly on your device. You can contact
[dev@timonply.com](mailto:dev@timonply.com) for privacy questions or requests.

Exactake does not currently use automated decision-making with legal or
similarly significant effects. Nutrition targets and progress summaries are
informational estimates only.

## 10. Children

Exactake is not directed to children under 16. If you are under the age required
by the laws of your country to use digital services without parental consent,
use the App only with permission from a parent or guardian.
