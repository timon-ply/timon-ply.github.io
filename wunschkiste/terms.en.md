## Visibility and your own images

Profile photos remain within your authorised account and are not published as gift-givers' pictures. Guests see a preset cover instead of your own box cover until the exact uploaded image version has been manually approved. A replacement needs new review. You can see an unapproved image in your authorised view. Approval does not promise that all rights are cleared or that content will remain available permanently.

Invitation codes and links grant access to people who know them; they can be forwarded. Share them only with intended guests. Wunschkiste cannot recall other people's screenshots, downloads or forwarded copies. For a child's birthday, you can use a preset illustration instead of a child's photo.

## Content you may supply

Supply only content that you may store, process and share for these purposes. Other people's works require sufficient permission from the rights holders; pictures of people require their necessary consent and, for children, the necessary permission of the responsible parent. A parent approval for a Wunschkiste account replaces neither these image rights nor other depicted people's permission. Do not upload confidential documents, private credentials or unnecessary personal information about others.

Prohibited content includes rights-infringing, discriminatory, harassing or threatening content, sexual exploitation and child sexual abuse material. Do not use boxes or invitations for fraud or to circumvent restrictions. Content may be restricted for review or removed for violations; access may be restricted. A word and link filter does not replace complete content review.

The current beta imports no retailer photographs or shop descriptions and does not retrieve product details automatically. Enter the wish, price and optional details yourself; a supported HTTPS shop link opens the retailer externally. Seeing a shop image online does not give permission to copy it. Wunschkiste's content approval does not grant those rights.

## Age groups and parent approval

Adults declare their age group in the supported flow. An own account at 13–17 requires recorded parent approval. Own photo uploads additionally require explicit, recorded permission from the parent for this upload use; an earlier general account approval does not grant it retrospectively. Without that photo permission, use preset illustrations. Under 13, use a responsible parent's account; that parent decides which content is supplied there and must hold the necessary rights and permissions.

In the supported family flow, the parent can explicitly grant or withdraw the additional photo permission. Photo withdrawal blocks future own photo uploads but does not automatically remove previously stored images. To remove existing images, use the available functions or the contact method below. General account approval and photo permission are separate decisions; photo permission does not establish rights to a particular image either.

Age statements, verified email and parent declarations are recorded statements. Wunschkiste does not claim independent verification of age, identity or parental authority. Account approval and its withdrawal are separate from permission concerning uploaded content and account deletion. Details are in [Help](/wunschkiste/help.en.html) and [Privacy](/wunschkiste/privacy.en.html).

You can replace or remove your own images through the available feature and request erasure of your associated account data. For questions about removal or ending this use, contact [dev@timonply.com](mailto:dev@timonply.com). Uninstalling, signing out or archiving a box does not erase its server data. Guests who lose device access may need to prove permission so that someone else's content is not erased.

The licence for ongoing display ends upon confirmed removal or erasure of the affected content. Where technically necessary backups remain until their regular overwrite, or bounded evidence is needed to handle a report, protect the service or defend legitimate claims, only the retention and processing necessary for those purposes remain covered; renewed publication or marketing use is excluded. Closed report receipts are intended for manual cleanup after 90 days; open cases remain until handled, with no automatic deletion job. Details and provider retention periods are in [Privacy](/wunschkiste/privacy.en.html) and [Delete account and data](/wunschkiste/deletion.en.html).

## Reports and contact

Report problematic content through “Report content” in a box or wish menu, or to [dev@timonply.com](mailto:dev@timonply.com). Identify the content and reason without sending passwords, private keys or complete identity documents. “Block creator” hides that creator's boxes for your access; it does not erase other people's content. Only a receipt confirmation shows that a report has arrived. Handling does not guarantee a particular legal assessment or immediate removal.
