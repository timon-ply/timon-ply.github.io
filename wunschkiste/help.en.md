# Help with Wunschkiste

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This website provides help and legal information. You can find the web view and Android direct download at [Wunschkiste](https://wunschkiste.timon-polley1.workers.dev/).

## Introduction

The first native iOS test version uses the same online boxes as the web view. These instructions distinguish shared features from older Android features. A test version does not demonstrate completed public store approval.

When you first open the app, it shows how to create a list, add wishes and share an invitation. Use “Next” or swipe to move between steps, go back, or skip at any time. You can then begin without an account, create an account or sign in. Open the steps again under “Profile → Help → View introduction”. You can expand individual topics in Help. Existing accounts and guest lists are retained when you update.

## Create a list

Create your wish list under “Lists”. Choose a name and cover image. A date and description are optional: they help guests understand the occasion and your wishes. Then add wishes and share the invitation.

## Start without an account

You can begin as a guest. Your editing permission remains on this device. The list itself is stored online so guests can view it. Clearing app data, uninstalling the app or losing the device may remove your management access. To use multiple devices, create an account and link your own guest lists through the available transfer feature. A guest invitation alone does not give you ownership of someone else's list.

## Use an account

New accounts use an email address and password. Choose “Create account”, enter your details and request a verification link. Open the link in your inbox, return to the app and choose “Check verification”. Then finish signing in. For an already verified account, sign in directly with your email address and password. You do not need a Google Account for this. You can still begin as a guest without an account.

If “Continue with Google” is displayed, you can alternatively sign in through Google's account dialog. “Continue with Apple” is only offered when configured and enabled for your app version; Apple's dialog opens in the browser. Return to the app afterwards and check completion there. After an interrupted connection, you can check a still-valid operation again. An expired operation requires a new start.

The new provider sign-in flow shows an explanation before each Apple/Google operation and requires explicit, initially unchecked consent. With the same verified email address, Firebase may use existing email/Google access, associate providers or replace a previously untrusted credential under its provider rules. Without consent, this new flow opens no provider dialog. Consent is not a promise that Firebase prevents every association; later cancellation does not automatically undo provider changes already made. You can use the offered email sign-in or guest mode instead.

With Apple, you can choose “Hide My Email”. Wunschkiste then receives the relay address instead of your real address. Actual message forwarding depends on Apple and your settings and is not guaranteed. For provider-only access, confirm sensitive actions through the associated provider; you do not need to invent a Wunschkiste password. Removing the app or disabling forwarding does not delete your Wunschkiste account.

After a successful sign-in, you remain signed in on this device while the session is valid. Use a unique password with at least 15 characters and save it in your password manager. Spaces remain part of the password. Keep access to your email inbox up to date so you can receive verification and recovery links.

You do not need to copy or export a long account key for a new account. An old account key you still have is private access, not an invitation code. Do not share it with guests.

Use the pencil icon in your profile to edit your name. “Save” confirms the name change. On iOS, a newly selected profile photo there is saved too; passwords are changed separately. If your account uses a password, “Change password” opens a separate dialog asking for your current and new passwords; an incorrect current password is rejected. For email accounts, other devices must enter their password again once their previous access expires; for older accounts, other sessions become invalid when the password changes. “Signed-in devices” is directly available in Profile.

In your profile, you can select, replace or remove your own profile photo. It remains private to your account and is not automatically shown to guests. Under “Language”, switch between German and English. Your choice is saved; product names and text you enter yourself are not translated.

## Continue using an older account

This section describes older Android access. For the first iOS version, first link such an account to a verified email address through the feature offered there. iOS offers the new email/provider sign-in, not legacy key sign-in or passkeys.

Your older account is retained. On the sign-in page, choose “Previous account with username” to use your existing access. Accounts without a configured password retain their old account key as access. While signed in to an older account, you can still set up a password under “Profile → Pencil icon → Set up password”.

To switch to email sign-in, choose “Set up email sign-in” in Profile. You can create new email access or choose “Use existing email sign-in”. Verify your email address and also confirm your previous password, or your previous account key if the account has no password. Your lists and reservations remain in the same Wunschkiste account. Your previous access remains valid until completion is confirmed.

After a successful switch, sign in with your email address and the password chosen for that access. Old account keys, passkeys and recovery codes are no longer valid. Older Wunschkiste accounts using usernames or account keys are not merged solely because names or email addresses match. This is separate from the Firebase provider association described above. You cannot additionally link email access that is already associated with another Wunschkiste account to your older account.

## Import product details

In the first iOS version, paste the link into the wish form. Enter the name, price and note yourself; size and colour are under further details. Shop data and external product photos are not imported automatically here. Neutral gift visuals replace retailer images until the necessary shop-specific rights and permissions are established. A wish can also have no link. Opening a link purchases only at the shop; Wunschkiste places no order. The automatic retrieval and sharing from other apps described below concern Android versions offering those features.

You can enter size and colour as optional details directly below the name. Guests see them in the app and browser. They do not split a wish into multiple shop variants.

Paste an HTTPS product address or share it to Wunschkiste from another app. Available names, images and prices are retrieved from supported shops. Some shops block retrieval or do not provide all details. Fill in missing information directly in the form. A wish can also be created without a product link. A displayed price is a snapshot; the price charged by the shop at purchase is what matters.

## Secure and recover your access

For an email account, choose “Forgot password” on the sign-in page, enter your email address and request a link. Open it in your inbox, set a new password and then sign in to the app again. If no message arrives, also check your spam folder and the spelling of the address. Confirmation of the request does not reveal whether an account exists for the address entered. Support cannot show you your password or replace proof of access to someone else's account.

For access that uses Google exclusively, use your Google Account's recovery features. Wunschkiste's password feature applies to accounts with a configured Wunschkiste password.

For Apple-only access, use your Apple Account's recovery and, where applicable, check “Hide My Email” settings. Sign in again with the Apple access belonging to the affected account. A new email address or new provider access does not prove ownership of another existing account.

Passkeys remain available under “Profile → Passkeys” for older accounts without an email link. They require Android 9 or newer and an available provider on your device. Wunschkiste does not create a provider account for you. Passkeys are not offered for email accounts in this version.

If you configured a recovery code for an older account, it remains usable until email access is linked. Under “Previous account with username → Recover access”, you can set a new password using your username and saved code. Then sign in normally; all previous sessions, passkeys and old access credentials become invalid. Set up any desired passkeys again afterwards. An older account without a linked email address cannot be recovered through an email link.

## Archive lists

On iOS, open the box and choose “Archive box” at the bottom. Open the archive through the menu in “My boxes”. In an archived box, “Restore box” returns it when an active slot is available.

Open the list and choose “Archive” while editing it. It then appears in the archive instead of among active lists. “Undo” or “Restore” brings back the same list. Invitation links and reservations remain valid while it is archived. Archiving is not deletion; duplication is not offered.

## Invite people and join a list

Open “Share” in your list. The dialog displays the invitation code prominently. “Copy code” copies it for the app; “Share link” opens the native share menu with the public web link. Older lists without a code can still be shared by link. New links open the web view at the new Wunschkiste address or open the list directly in an installed, associated Android app. Old application links on this website remain removed; existing lists' invitation codes remain usable.

Under “Gifts”, enter an invitation code. The preview first shows the list. Only “Join” adds it to your invitations. First check whether its name, date and description match the expected occasion. Invitation links and codes give access to people who know them; do not publish them unintentionally.

## Choose a gift

Reserve an available wish before buying it. Other guests then see that it is taken. Gifts are anonymous by default. If the list allows name sharing, you can voluntarily agree to display your name as a signed-in guest. Without your explicit agreement, it remains hidden. Your email address and profile photo are not shown. Purchases take place in the shop. After buying, you can mark the wish as purchased or release a reservation you no longer need. Without an account, editing your reservation depends on the original device or browser.

## Set surprise and anonymity preferences

While editing your list, you can enable “Hide reservations from me”. In your signed-in app, you then cannot see which wishes are reserved or purchased. This also applies to related notifications. The public guest view still shows availability to guests; the setting does not make the invitation secret.

“Anonymous gifts” protects gift-givers' names. If it is switched off, guests may only display their name with their own agreement. Already anonymous reservations do not acquire a visible name as a result. Share invitation codes and links only with the intended people.

## Use notifications

On iOS, change device permission in iOS settings. Banners stay generic: no gift-giver, item or box names. Messages already handed to the delivery service may still appear after disabling notifications or signing out; the short requested expiry does not promise immediate recall. Previously confirmed activation can be restored on the next launch without a new permission prompt.

Under “Profile → Notifications”, you can individually enable or disable push notifications about new guests and purchased wishes. Both switches start off. Independently of the push switches, the activity overview shows the latest, up to 20 actual events in your lists. The switches do not delete or disable this overview.

Push to your device is only offered if delivery has been configured. Deliberately enable it and, if needed, allow notifications in Android. No push notifications appear without device permission. Push messages contain no gift-givers' names or photos. You can change permission in Android's settings at any time. Existing notifications may remain in the app when push is switched off.

## Free usage and Premium

For free, you can create up to three active lists with up to 20 active wishes each. Archived lists do not count as active. Existing content saved before this limit remains retained and accessible. When a limit has been reached, you can edit, remove or archive existing content; new content requires an available slot. Invitations and reservations remain usable.

Premium is a planned offering. There is currently no purchase, subscription or checkout. A preview does not unlock features or trigger payment. Google Analytics is not used.

At a limit, you can select an active list to archive or a wish to remove. The form is preserved. The new version also prepares voluntary AdMob videos for adults: a confirmed video may unlock one extra active-list slot or five extra wish slots for the selected list. At most three extra list slots and 20 extra wish slots per list are planned, with at most one reward per account per UTC calendar day. Only verified server confirmation counts, not closing the video. If no ad is available, normal archive or removal recovery remains available. Production ads and videos are currently off and additionally require confirmed store/provider review and technical readiness. The published German/English consent message for all regions offers a direct decline option; publication alone does not activate ads.

## Age group and advertising

This section and the prepared reward videos above concern Android. The first iOS version has no advertising integration, reward videos, purchasable Premium or age-group selector. Archive boxes or remove wishes there to free capacity.

The app is intended for adults and children. The prepared advertising feature neutrally asks for “Under 13”, “13–17” or “18+” before initial consent/advertising startup. There is no default choice. Without a choice and under 18, neither UMP nor the advertising SDK starts; there are no banners or reward videos. Normal app features remain usable. The choice stores only the coarse category locally on this device, without a date of birth or cloud transmission; it is excluded from device backups.

Change the choice under “Profile → Age group” or in settings, or cancel selection for now. Changing it hides banners; further ad requests remain blocked until a permitted new choice. Adults additionally require provider readiness and consent checks. Outstanding server confirmation for a previously started optional video remains checkable; choosing an age never grants extra capacity itself. The standalone guest web view contains no ads.

## Select your own cover image

Use a provided design or an image you are permitted to share. Invited people can also see the cover image. A design is enough for a child's birthday; a child's photo is not needed. The app prepares selected images for upload. If an upload is rejected, the existing list is preserved; choose a smaller image or a provided design.

## If something does not work

If saving a new wish leaves its outcome uncertain, inspect the box first. Do not blindly repeat creation; the draft is retained for the offered check and a deliberately confirmed retry.

On iOS, guest and account access is stored in the device-bound Keychain. “Sign out” ends account access; guest permissions may remain. Do not rely on uninstalling as server deletion or as reliable removal of all Keychain data. During an unfinished deletion, retain the app and access until the server confirms completion.

Check your connection and try the action again. A change is only saved once confirmed by the server. Limits of the free test operation may temporarily prevent further actions. A reservation or repeated save attempt does not place an order. This version does not offer purchases or subscriptions.

Confirm account deletion in Profile using your current access. If “Account is being deleted” appears, the process has not yet been fully completed. Choose “Continue deletion” once a connection is available again. Do not clear app data during this process: the confirmation is kept on this device so deletion can continue. Only the final confirmation means that the server has completed the process.

An Apple-associated account may require fresh confirmation with the same Apple access and provider revocation before deletion. Follow the offered Apple confirmation instead of creating a new account. Your general Apple Account remains; the completion message does not promise instant deletion of all data or backups held by Apple or Google.

For lost access, deletion requests or other problems, contact [dev@timonply.com](mailto:dev@timonply.com). Mention the app, your device and the affected action; do not send passwords or private keys. Information on removing data is available under [Delete account and data](/wunschkiste/deletion.en.html).
