Visibility and your own images

Profile photos remain within your authorised account and are not published as gift-givers' pictures. Guests see a preset cover instead of your own box cover until the exact uploaded image version has been manually approved. Own wish photos use the same review of the exact image digest and revision; guests see a neutral visual until approval. A replacement needs new review. You can see an unapproved image in your authorised view. Approval does not promise that all rights are cleared or that content will remain available permanently.

Invitation codes and links grant access to people who know them; they can be forwarded. Share them only with intended guests. Wunschkiste cannot recall other people's screenshots, downloads or forwarded copies. For a child's birthday, you can use a preset illustration instead of a child's photo.

Age groups and parent approval

Adults declare their age group in the supported flow. An own account at 13–17 requires recorded parent approval. Own photo uploads additionally require explicit, recorded permission from the parent for this upload use; an earlier general account approval does not grant it retrospectively. Without that photo permission, use preset illustrations. Under 13, use a responsible parent's account; that parent decides which content is supplied there and must hold the necessary rights and permissions.

In the supported family flow, the parent can explicitly grant or withdraw the additional photo permission. Photo withdrawal blocks future own photo uploads but does not automatically remove previously stored images. To remove existing images, use the available functions or the contact method below. General account approval and photo permission are separate decisions; photo permission does not establish rights to a particular image either.

Age statements, verified email and parent declarations are recorded statements. Wunschkiste does not claim independent verification of age, identity or parental authority. Account approval and its withdrawal are separate from permission concerning uploaded content and account deletion. Details are in Help and Privacy.

Removal and end of use

You can replace or remove your own images through the available feature and request erasure of your associated account data. For questions about removal or ending this use, contact dev@timonply.com. Uninstalling, signing out or archiving a box does not erase its server data. Guests who lose device access may need to prove permission so that someone else's content is not erased.

The licence for ongoing display ends upon confirmed removal or erasure of the affected content. Where technically necessary backups remain until their regular overwrite, or bounded evidence is needed to handle a report, protect the service or defend legitimate claims, only the retention and processing necessary for those purposes remain covered; renewed publication or marketing use is excluded. Closed report receipts are intended for manual cleanup after 90 days; open cases remain until handled, with no automatic deletion job. Details and provider retention periods are in Privacy and Delete account and data.

After confirmed image removal, the private image store may retain empty objects with zero image bytes and bounded technical deletion fences. They prevent a delayed upload from restoring a removed image under the same object identifier. These records may include internal owner, list/account, resource and object identifiers and bounded status and capacity data; the fence contains no image content, names or email addresses and cannot sign in to or restore a deleted account. Historically occupied object slots are not released by removing an image. These technical fences currently have no automatic expiry, so complete removal of every technical reference or later release of all capacity is not promised. Contact the controller with questions about their continued necessity or erasure.

Reports and contact

Identify the content and reason without sending passwords, private keys or complete identity documents. “Block creator” hides that creator's boxes for your access; it does not erase other people's content. Only a receipt confirmation shows that a report has arrived. Handling does not guarantee a particular legal assessment or immediate removal.
