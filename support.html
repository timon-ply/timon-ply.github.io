Email support

Support intake

What to include

  • App name: Exactake, Flatnest, Kalvenda, or NeonRoutine
  • Platform: Android, iOS, web, or desktop
  • App version and build number, if available
  • A short description of the issue or request
  • Steps that reproduce the problem, if this is a bug report
  • Relevant screenshots or error messages, with private data removed where possible
  • For safety reports, the group, chat, event, flat, invite, or profile context involved

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment credentials, full government IDs, or unrelated sensitive data by email.
